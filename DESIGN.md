# Design

## Direction

Preserve RolePatch’s black application-workbench language: high-contrast white type, restrained indigo evidence accents, compact product navigation, and receipt-first copy.

## Rules

- Public reading surfaces use the existing Astro landing tokens and typography.
- Emphasize review, evidence, and safety boundaries rather than automation spectacle.
- Keep release entries chronological with generous separation and readable measures.
- Navigation may wrap on mobile but cannot clip or hide the Dashboard path.
